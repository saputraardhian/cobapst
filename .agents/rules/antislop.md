# antislop: Rules for AI Coding Agents

> Source: https://github.com/miqdadbadjuber/anti-slop.git

## Purpose
A filter that stops AI coding agents from producing generic, filler UI, fake numbers, and AI-shaped code.

## The Craftsmanship Standard
1. **C-1: Intentionality** - Every visual and copy decision has a reason you can articulate.
2. **C-2: Functional Completeness** - Every interactive element works, or it does not exist.
3. **C-3: Content-Driven Composition** - Every section exists because the product content needs it.
4. **C-4: Resilience** - The UI holds up in every state, theme, breakpoint, and keyboard use.
5. **C-5: Evidence Over Claims** - Facts, statistics, and government claims must be real, verifiable, and consistent.

## Hard Gates
- No fake metrics or invented hype numbers.
- No generic AI sparkle emojis or AI buzzwords.
- No technique without purpose (purpose-gate: gradients, shadows, borders must serve hierarchy and legibility).
- High contrast ratio (WCAG AA) on text and backgrounds.
- Pure semantic HTML, clean responsive layouts, and zero-slop code comments.
